Concept C: enhanced

Everything in Concept B, plus new ideas
  1. Cairo typefaceOne typeface for Arabic and English, with a consistent rhythm across both.
  2. Care navigatorTwo questions (who? what do you need?) route visitors to the right next step. Urgent needs turn red and point to 997.
  3. Live ER wait timesA live board of the nearest emergency rooms with a busy indicator. Needs a real data feed; illustrative here.
  4. Instant searchA unified search palette for doctors, specialties, facilities and services; opens from the search icon or Ctrl+K.
  5. Life-stage journeysPregnancy, children, adults and seniors, each with recommended steps and a direct booking action.
  6. Health calendarThe year's campaigns in one strip, which also gives the comms team a planning view.
  7. Know your numbersAn interactive BMI calculator plus blood-pressure and blood-sugar reference values, each linked to a booking.
  8. Ask EHC assistantA chat assistant mock-up with scripted replies. It could later be connected to EHC's knowledge base.
  9. Careers & volunteeringTwo cards to attract talent and volunteers.
